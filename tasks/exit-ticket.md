# Exit ticket (5 minutes)

Answer on your own, one or two sentences each, and hand it in where the instructor says.

1. Your push is rejected because your partner pushed first. What do you do, and which "fix" must you never accept from an agent? Why?
2. An agent changed seven files and says all tests pass. Name two things you check before you commit.
3. Why does `AGENTS.md` belong in git rather than only on your laptop?
4. Which rung of the trust ladder would you allow an agent on your final project, and on which branches?
